Booking Transport Southampton

Wazer Taxis

A local taxi and transport operator in and around Southampton, working in a category where national apps dominate the app stores and the paid ads.

Trade
Taxi & transport
Area served
Southampton
What the site had to do
Booking
What we built
Booking and phone contact on every page, service breakdown, local search structure
STEP 01

The brief

Wazer Taxis provides local taxi and transport services in and around Southampton, competing directly against national ride-booking apps that dominate app-store listings and paid advertising. A firm like this has a strong base of repeat customers with the number already saved — the opportunity is the passenger who searches “taxi Southampton” and expects to find a bookable local option in seconds.

The brief was a site that makes booking a ride as fast as calling one, sets out clearly what the firm actually offers, and gives a first-time passenger the reassurance of a real, established local operator.

STEP 02

The build

We placed contact options — phone number and booking request — prominently on every page, with service information laid out clearly so passengers know what’s available before they get in touch: local journeys, airport transfers, advance bookings.

The whole site is structured around local search, with content and metadata built specifically for “taxi Southampton” and related terms, so people looking for a ride in the area can find Wazer directly rather than defaulting to a national app by habit.

STEP 03

The outcome

Wazer Taxis has a site where service information and a clear way to book are visible immediately, with no hunting for a phone number or guessing what’s on offer.

Because the structure is built around local Southampton search terms, it gives the firm a genuine chance of being found by passengers searching in the area rather than losing that search to a national app — and gives first-time passengers the reassurance of a proper web presence behind the phone number.
