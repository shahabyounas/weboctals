Before you ask

Three things we won’t tell you

Agencies get a bad name for a reason. Here’s where we’ll disagree with the pitch you’ve probably had before.

That we can guarantee a Google ranking

Nobody can, and Google’s own guidance on hiring an SEO lists a guaranteed number-one ranking as a reason to walk away. We build the structure that makes ranking possible. The ranking itself isn’t ours to sell.

That you need AI in this

Plenty of projects don’t. If yours is one, we’ll build it without — a cheaper project for you and a better result, and we’d rather have the repeat work than the bigger invoice.

That a website will fix the business

Sometimes the site isn’t what’s holding you back — it’s pricing, availability or reviews. We’ll say that before quoting, even when the honest answer costs us the job.

Questions

Straight answers

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Mostly small UK businesses that aren’t tech companies: driving schools, takeaways, barbers, taxi firms, trade suppliers, a currency exchange and a lighting studio, all of them written up.

We also build the harder end — AI agents, automation pipelines and custom models — for teams who’ve outgrown off-the-shelf tools.

Who will I actually be talking to?

The people building it. There’s no account layer relaying messages and nothing is outsourced, which is why scoping tends to be quick and estimates hold up.

What does a project cost, and how long does it take?

There’s no fixed price list, because a single-location business and a multi-town operation aren’t the same job. We scope it, quote a fixed figure in pounds, and you see that number before any work begins.

A typical small business website is two to four weeks depending on scope. Agents and automation are quoted case by case. Either way you get a timeline before anything starts.

Can you guarantee we’ll rank on Google?

No, and you should treat anyone who says otherwise as a warning sign — Google says exactly that in its own guidance on hiring an SEO.

What can be done is structural: pages built around the places you genuinely serve, valid local business markup, a site that’s fast on mobile data, and a Google Business Profile that matches. That’s the work.

Do I actually need AI?

Often not. If a problem doesn’t need it we’ll build it without, which is a cheaper project and usually a better result.

Where it earns its place is the unglamorous work: the agent that handles the fiftieth identical enquiry, the automation that removes an afternoon of copy-paste, the model that catches what a person would have missed.

Do you work with businesses outside the UK?

Our work so far is all UK, and pricing is quoted in pounds. The build itself is done remotely, so being near us has never been part of how a project works — tell us where you are and we’ll be straight about whether we’re a good fit.
