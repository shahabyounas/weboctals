Before you ask

Three things we’ll be straight with you about

Here’s where we’ll part company with the pitch you’ve probably heard before.

Where a Google ranking actually comes from

Google’s own guidance on hiring an SEO treats a guaranteed number-one ranking as a reason to walk away, and we agree with it. We build the structure that makes ranking possible — the pages, the markup, the speed — and the ranking follows from the work.

Whether your project needs AI

Plenty of projects are stronger built the simple way — less to go wrong, less to maintain, and a result you can reason about. Where yours is one of them we’ll say so, because we’d rather build the thing you still rely on in two years than the thing that demos well.

What a website can move on its own

Sometimes the real lever isn’t the site at all — it’s your availability, your reviews, or how fast the phone gets answered. We’ll tell you that up front, even when the straight answer costs us the job, because that’s the conversation worth having.

Questions

Straight answers

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Mostly small UK businesses outside the tech world: driving schools, takeaways, barbers, taxi firms, trade suppliers, a currency exchange and a lighting studio, all of them written up.

We also build the harder end — AI agents, automation pipelines and custom models — for teams who’ve outgrown off-the-shelf tools.

Who will I actually be talking to?

The people building it. You speak directly with the engineer writing your code, and the whole build stays in-house — which is why scoping tends to be quick and estimates hold up.

How do you scope a project, and how long does it take?

We start from the job the thing has to do — take the booking at 11pm on a Sunday, take the order, save the afternoon of copy-paste. Every feature is measured against that job, and the ones that don’t serve it don’t get built. A single-location business and a multi-town operation are different jobs, so the scope is written for yours and you see it before any work begins.

A typical small business website runs two to four weeks depending on scope. Agents and automation are scoped case by case. Either way you get a timeline before anything starts.

Can you guarantee we’ll rank on Google?

Treat a guaranteed ranking as a warning sign from anyone who offers one — Google says exactly that in its own guidance on hiring an SEO.

What we can do is structural: pages built around the places you genuinely serve, valid local business markup, a site that’s fast on mobile data, and a Google Business Profile that matches. That’s the work, and it’s what makes a ranking reachable.

Do I actually need AI?

Often the simpler build wins. Where that’s true of your project we’ll build it that way — less to go wrong, and usually the result that holds up longer.

Where AI earns its place is the unglamorous work: the agent that handles the fiftieth identical enquiry, the automation that saves an afternoon of copy-paste, the model that catches what a person would have missed.

Do you work with businesses outside the UK?

Our work so far is all UK. The build itself is done remotely, so distance has always been beside the point — tell us where you are and we’ll be straight about whether we’re a good fit.
