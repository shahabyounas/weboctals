Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about WebOctals AI solutions

How long does an AI project take?

Project timelines vary based on complexity and scope. AI agent developments typically take 6-12 weeks, while comprehensive digital products may take 3-6 months. Simple chatbot implementations can be completed in 2-4 weeks. We provide detailed timelines during our initial consultation based on your specific requirements.

Do you work with small businesses?

Absolutely! We work with businesses of all sizes, from startups to enterprises. Our AI solutions are scalable and tailored to fit your budget and needs. We offer flexible pricing packages for small businesses, including phased implementation options that allow you to start small and scale as you grow.

What industries do you serve?

We serve various industries including healthcare, finance, e-commerce, manufacturing, retail, hospitality, and professional services. Our AI solutions are adaptable to most business sectors. We have expertise in building custom AI agents, chatbots, automation systems, and loyalty programs across diverse market verticals.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes, we offer comprehensive support packages including maintenance, updates, optimization, and troubleshooting to ensure your AI solutions perform at their best. Our support plans include 24/7 monitoring, regular performance reviews, feature enhancements, and dedicated technical assistance to keep your systems running smoothly.

How much do AI solutions cost?

Pricing varies based on project complexity, features, and scale. Basic AI chatbot implementations start from $5,000, while custom AI agent systems range from $15,000 to $100,000+. We offer flexible payment plans and can provide a detailed quote after understanding your specific requirements during an initial consultation.

What AI technologies do you use?

We work with cutting-edge AI technologies including OpenAI GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, and other leading Large Language Models (LLMs). Our tech stack includes Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch, LangChain, and modern frameworks for building robust AI agents, chatbots, automation systems, and machine learning solutions tailored to your business needs.
