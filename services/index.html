How a project runs

The same four steps, whichever service it is

STEP 01

Describe the problem

Not the solution. Half the value of the first conversation is working out which service — if any — actually applies.

STEP 02

Scope against the outcome

Scoped against the number that has to move — bookings, orders, hours saved. Anything that doesn’t serve it gets cut, and you see the scope before any work begins.

STEP 03

Build, in the open

You talk to whoever is building it. Nothing is outsourced, so there’s no one to relay questions through and no one to point at.

STEP 04

Stay after launch

Monitoring, iteration and support once it’s live. A thing that shipped and then quietly broke is worse than no thing at all.
