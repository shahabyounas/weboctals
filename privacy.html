Privacy Policy

What we collect when you visit or contact us, why we collect it, and what you can do about it — in plain English first, full detail below.

Effective date: 10 August 2026

The gist

  • We're WebOctals Ltd, a UK company. When you fill in our contact form, we collect what you type (name, email, message, etc.) plus some automatic technical details (your rough location, device and browser) so we can reply properly and stop spam.
  • We use Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager to see how the site is used — but only after you accept our cookie banner. Reject it and those tools don't load.
  • We share data only with the tools that run our business — Google (Sheets, Analytics, Tag Manager) and an IP-lookup service. We do not sell your data or hand it to marketing partners.
  • If you're in the UK or EU, you have full GDPR rights — access, correction, deletion, and more. If you're in the US, email us and we'll help even where local law doesn't strictly require it.
  • Questions? Email contact@weboctals.co.uk.

1. Who we are

This site is operated by WEBOCTALS LTD ("WebOctals", "we", "us", "our"), a company registered in England and Wales under company number 16730955, registered office 54 Dorset Terrace, Leeds, England, LS8 3QR.

For the purposes of UK GDPR and EU GDPR, WebOctals is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at contact@weboctals.co.uk or +44 7442 410345 for anything to do with your data.

2. What we collect

We keep this to what we actually collect on this site — nothing more.

Information you give us

When you use our contact or newsletter forms, we collect whatever you enter: name, email address, company name, phone number, the service you're interested in, budget range, and your message.

Information collected automatically

When you submit a form, we also automatically capture: your device type, browser and operating system, screen size, the page that referred you, your full browser user-agent string, and — via an IP-lookup service (ipapi.co) — your IP address and approximate country and city. We use this to sanity-check enquiries, spot spam, and understand who's getting in touch.

Information from cookies and analytics

If you accept our cookie banner, Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager collect standard usage data — pages viewed, buttons clicked, session length, and similar. See section 4 for details and how to opt out.

What we don't collect

We don't collect payment card details (we don't take payments on this site), and we don't ask for sensitive categories of data such as health, religion or political opinions.

TypePurposeLoads without consent?

You can change your mind at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this site, or by using the "manage cookie preferences" option where it appears in the banner.

5. Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal data, and we don't share it with third parties for their own marketing. We do share it with a small number of service providers ("processors") who help us run the business:

  • Google — Google Sheets and Google Apps Script (to store and process contact form submissions), Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager (site usage analytics, only with consent).
  • ipapi.co — a geolocation lookup service we use to turn an IP address into an approximate country/city on form submission.
  • Our hosting and email providers, to the extent needed to run the website and respond to you.

We may also disclose data where required by law, for example in response to a valid request from a court or regulator.

6. International transfers

Some of the processors above (particularly Google) may process data outside the UK and European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where that happens, we rely on mechanisms recognised under UK GDPR and EU GDPR, such as Standard Contractual Clauses and the providers' own data protection commitments, to keep your data protected to a comparable standard.

7. How long we keep it

We keep contact form and enquiry data for as long as needed to respond to you, and afterwards for a reasonable period for our own business records — typically no longer than 24 months, unless we're required to keep it longer (for example, for accounting purposes) or you ask us to delete it sooner.

Analytics data collected via Google Analytics follows Google's own retention settings, which default to 14 months, unless we've configured our account differently.

8. Your rights

If you're in the UK or EU

Under UK GDPR and/or EU GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it (access)
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data (rectification)
  • Ask us to delete your data (erasure), subject to legal exceptions
  • Ask us to restrict how we use your data
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Receive your data in a portable format, where technically feasible
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting anything done before you withdrew it
  • Complain to your national data protection authority — in the UK, that's the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)

To exercise any of these, email contact@weboctals.co.uk. We'll respond within one month.

If you're in the United States

We don't sell or share your personal information with third parties for their own use, so there's no "opt out of sale" mechanism to offer. If you'd like to know what we hold about you, or want it deleted, email us at contact@weboctals.co.uk and we'll help — regardless of which US state you're in.

9. Children's privacy

This site is intended for businesses and adults, not children. We don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13 (or the minimum age that applies where you live, if higher). If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the data we hold, including access controls and secure transmission (HTTPS). No system is completely immune to risk, but we take reasonable steps to keep your data safe and would notify you and, where required, the ICO in the event of a breach affecting your data.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our tools or practices change. We'll update the effective date at the top of this page when we do. Significant changes will be flagged clearly on the site.
