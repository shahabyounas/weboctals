What we do

Most of our clients aren’t tech companies

They’re driving schools, chip shops, barbers, taxi firms and trade suppliers — businesses where a website isn’t a brand exercise, it’s the thing that takes the booking at 11pm on a Sunday.

That shapes how we build. We start from the job the site has to do, keep the stack boring where boring is correct, and use AI where it genuinely removes work — answering the repetitive enquiry, sorting the order, flagging the pattern nobody had time to look for.

We also build the harder end: AI agents, automation pipelines and custom models for teams who’ve outgrown off-the-shelf tools. Same principle either way — it has to earn its place. You can see everything we offer, or go straight to the work itself.

How we work

Commitments we’ll hold ourselves to

And that you can hold us to. These are the ones we’d want to be judged on.

01

You talk to the people building it

No account layer relaying messages. The engineer writing your code is in the conversation, which is why scoping is quick and estimates hold up.

02

Scoped against an outcome

Every project starts with the number that has to move — bookings, enquiries, hours saved. If a feature doesn’t serve it, we’ll say so before it’s built.

03

Built in-house, start to finish

Nothing is outsourced. That keeps quality consistent and means there’s no one to point at when something needs fixing — it’s us.

04

We stay after launch

Monitoring, iteration and support once it’s live. A site that shipped and then quietly broke is worse than no site at all.

Where we stand on AI

Useful beats impressive

Plenty of AI work is a demo that never survives contact with real users. We’re interested in the unglamorous version: the agent that handles the fiftieth identical enquiry, the automation that removes an afternoon of copy-paste, the model that catches what a person would have missed.

If a problem doesn’t need AI, we’ll build it without — less to go wrong, less to maintain, and usually the result that holds up longer. We’d rather build the thing you still rely on in two years than the thing that demos well.
