Before you write

Straight answers

How quickly will I hear back?

One of our engineers replies within one working day. If it’s urgent, WhatsApp or phone will always be faster than a form.

How do you decide what to build?

We start from the outcome you need — the booking taken, the order placed, the afternoon of admin saved. Every feature is measured against that outcome, and the ones that don’t serve it don’t get built. A single-location business and a multi-town operation need different things, so the scope is written for yours and you see it before any work begins.

If the job needs to be smaller, say so in the first message. It’s better to scope down deliberately than to discover the mismatch three emails in.

How long does a build take?

A typical small business website is two to four weeks depending on scope. Agents, automation and custom product work are scoped case by case, and you get a timeline before anything starts.

Search visibility runs on a separate and slower clock — new pages have to be crawled and indexed first, and nobody can put a date on that.

Do you work with small businesses?

Almost entirely. Most of what we build is for small UK businesses — driving schools, takeaways, barbers, taxi firms, trade suppliers. Scope flexes to fit, including phased builds that start with the piece that earns its keep first and add more once it has.

What if I’m not sure what I need?

That’s a normal first message. Describe what’s actually going wrong rather than the solution you think you need, and we’ll tell you which of our services fits — including telling you that none of them do, if that’s the honest answer.

Where are you based, and does it matter?

We’re UK-based and work remotely, so being near us has never been part of how a project runs. What matters is whether we understand the job your business needs doing.
