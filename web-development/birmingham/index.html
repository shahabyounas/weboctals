We are a Leeds studio and the work is done remotely, which is the same arrangement those clients have. There is no Birmingham office. What there is, is a track record in the region and a fair idea of how buyers here search, which is not quite how they search anywhere else.

The West Midlands has more trade, commercial and light-industrial businesses per square mile than most of the country, and those sell differently from a consumer trade. A racking supplier is not found the way a barber is found, and the site has to be built for the difference.

Quick Fit Shelving is in Wednesbury, which has a Walsall postcode and sits in Sandwell council. Three different answers to “where are you”, and the site has to pick the right one for each place it appears.

Coverage

Birmingham and the six boroughs around it

The West Midlands metropolitan county is seven councils and roughly 2.9 million people. Birmingham is the biggest local authority in the country by population, and it still accounts for well under half of the region.

One council, over a million people, and by some distance the most contested search market in the Midlands.

Four councils that share a border with Birmingham and almost nothing else. People here identify by town, firmly, and a business that calls itself Birmingham loses them.

Both inside the county, both distinct markets. Coventry in particular is a city in its own right and should never be swept into a Birmingham page.

Outside the metropolitan county but inside most firms’ real delivery radius, especially for trade and commercial supply.

For a supplier that delivers, the honest catchment usually runs on the motorway network rather than the county boundary — the M6, M5, M42 and M54 decide what is a same-day job far more than the map does.

Calling a Black Country business a Birmingham business loses both audiences: the locals who identify by town, and the Birmingham searchers who will pick someone actually in Birmingham.

The mistake we see most in this region

Local search

Two different sites, depending on who is buying

The West Midlands runs two local search markets side by side, and they need opposite page structures. Most sites here are built for one and sold to the other.

01

The consumer trade sells on place

A barber, a takeaway, a driving instructor or a fitter is chosen by proximity. The queries are place-first — the town, then the service — and the structure that wins is a page for each town you cover, plus a Google Business Profile whose service areas say the same thing.

02

The commercial supplier sells on product

A buyer sourcing pallet racking, shelving or fabrication searches the product and qualifies the area afterwards: the product, then “West Midlands” or “Black Country”. That needs a page per product line, with coverage stated on each, rather than a page per town.

03

The postcode will mislead you either way

WS10 is Wednesbury, which is Sandwell council, not Walsall. B69 is Oldbury, also Sandwell. B91 is Solihull, a different council again. Build the area pages on councils and towns; the letters at the front of a postcode are a sorting office, not a market.

04

Say the town before the city

In the Black Country the town name carries more trust than the city does, and it is a cheaper phrase to rank for. Lead with Oldbury or Halesowen or Bilston, then let Birmingham be one of the places you also serve, rather than the identity of the whole site.

Work out which of the two you are before the first page is drawn. It changes the sitemap, not just the wording.

Questions

Straight answers

Do you have West Midlands clients?

Yes. Quick Fit Shelving in Wednesbury is the one with a full case study and a screenshot; Lumenlux and Ariana Kitchens & Bedrooms are also West Midlands businesses and both have pages on the case studies, marked as portfolio entries rather than documented engagements.

That is the honest version. It is a real regional track record, and it is the reason this page can talk about how buyers here search without guessing.

Are you based in Birmingham?

No. We are a Leeds studio working remotely, and there is no Birmingham office and no West Midlands staff. The clients in the region work with us the same way every other client does: a call, a written scope, and direct access to the person building it.

If a project needs a face-to-face start, Leeds to Birmingham is a two-hour train and we will make the trip.

I am in the Black Country. Should the site say Birmingham?

Not as its identity. Lead with the town — Oldbury, Wednesbury, Halesowen, Bilston, Willenhall — because that is what local customers type and what they trust, and it is a far less contested phrase than the city.

Then include Birmingham as one of the areas you serve, on its own page, if you genuinely serve it. That way you hold both without pretending to be somewhere you are not.

Do you work with trade and commercial businesses, not just consumer ones?

Yes, and they get a different site. A commercial supplier needs a page per product line with capability, stock position and delivery coverage answered on it, because a trade buyer qualifies you in about thirty seconds and leaves if the page cannot do it.

Quick Fit Shelving is built that way, and there is a trade suppliers page that goes through the structure in full.

What does a website cost?

Websites are priced by page count: £1,000 up to five pages, £1,500 up to ten, and £2,000+ for twenty or more. Anything not measured in pages — an AI agent, an automation, a change to a site that already exists — is £50 an hour.

All of it is negotiable and all of it is fixed in writing before work starts. The full breakdown, including what counts as a page, is on the pricing page.

Can you guarantee I’ll rank first for “web design Birmingham”?

No, and treat anyone who does as a warning sign — Google’s own guidance on hiring an SEO lists a guaranteed number one ranking as a reason to walk away. Birmingham is one of the largest commercial search markets in the UK, and for most local businesses it is not where the enquiries are anyway.

What we can do is structural: a page for each area you genuinely serve, service-area markup that matches your Google Business Profile, a site that loads fast on mobile data, and internal links that let all of it be found. That is what makes a ranking reachable. The rest is Google’s call.

How long does a build take?

A typical small business website runs two to four weeks depending on scope. Agents and automation are scoped case by case. You get a timeline alongside the quote, before anything begins.

Search visibility runs on a separate and slower clock, because new pages have to be crawled and indexed before they can rank, and nobody can put a date on that.
