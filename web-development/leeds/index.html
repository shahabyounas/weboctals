Where we help

What Leeds businesses come to us for

Leeds runs on professional services, healthcare, retail and a wide belt of trades across the district. Those four buy very different software, and the difference shows up in the first conversation rather than at the end.

01

Legal, financial and professional services

Leeds carries the largest concentration of legal and financial services outside London, and most of the work there is document-shaped. Client onboarding that does not need re-keying, secure file handling, matter or case portals, and forms that capture the detail properly the first time.

02

Healthcare, clinics and care providers

Appointment booking that reflects real availability, referral and intake forms, staff and rota tooling, and anything touching patient data handled with the care that implies. Built to fit the systems a practice already runs rather than replacing them.

03

Retail, hospitality and consumer brands

Ordering and table booking that works on a phone at the moment someone decides, product pages that know what is in stock, and the automation that stops order admin eating an afternoon a day.

04

Trades and field services across the district

A page for each town you actually cover — Otley, Wetherby, Morley, Pudsey — rather than one homepage hoping to carry all of them, plus quote requests that arrive with enough detail to price without a second call.

If your business is none of those, it is still likely to be one of the shapes underneath: something to book, something to order, something to quote, or a manual process worth removing. That is the whole catalogue.

Process

How a project runs

The same four steps whether you are in Leeds or anywhere else in the country.

Step 01

A call, and a written scope

We start from the one job the thing has to do — take the booking, take the order, stop the afternoon of copy-paste. Every feature gets measured against that job, and the ones that do not serve it do not get built.

Step 02

A fixed price and a date

Both in writing before any work starts. A page-priced build does not turn into an hourly one halfway through, and the scope you approved is the scope you get.

Step 03

Built where you can watch it

It goes up on a real URL as it is made, and you talk to the engineer writing it rather than to an account manager relaying it. Changes are cheapest while it is still being built, which is the point.

Step 04

Live, indexed and handed over

Launch, sitemap submitted, structured data valid, and the page structure documented so you or anyone after us can extend it without guessing.

Questions

Straight answers

Are you based in Leeds?

Yes. WebOctals is a Leeds business. We work as a service-area business, so there is no shopfront and no street address published here — the registered address sits with Google and stays hidden, which is what Google recommends for a business that goes to its customers.

In practice that means a call for most projects, and a meeting in person wherever it genuinely helps, anywhere in the district or the wider county.

What can you build for a Leeds business?

Websites, online booking and ordering systems, customer portals, AI agents that handle repetitive enquiries, automation that removes manual data entry, and custom software for teams who have outgrown an off-the-shelf tool.

The starting question is always the same: what is the one job this has to do? Everything gets scoped against that answer, which is why the estimates hold.

How much does a website cost in Leeds?

The same as it costs anywhere else in the UK: £1,000 for up to five pages, £1,500 for up to ten, and £2,000+ for twenty or more. Work that is not measured in pages — an AI agent, an automation, a change to a site that already exists — is £50 an hour.

We do not price by postcode. Everything is negotiable, and whatever is agreed is fixed in writing before work starts. The pricing page sets out what counts as a page and what moves a quote either way.

How long does a website take to build?

Two to four weeks for a typical small business website, depending on how many pages there are and how quickly content comes back. AI agents, automation and custom software are scoped case by case, and you get a date alongside the quote before anything begins.

Search visibility runs on a slower and separate clock: new pages have to be crawled and indexed before they can rank, and nobody can put a date on that.

Can we meet in person?

Yes, anywhere in Leeds or West Yorkshire. It is genuinely useful at the start of a larger project, and unnecessary for most smaller ones — we will tell you honestly which yours is.

Nothing about the price or the process changes either way.

Which towns should my Leeds business target online?

The ones you would actually drive to on a Tuesday. For most local businesses that is between three and eight places, each with a page carrying something specific to it: what you do there, how long you have done it, what a job in that town usually involves.

A short list with real content in each outperforms a long coverage list of near-identical pages, which search engines treat as thin and customers see through just as quickly.

Can you guarantee first place for “web design Leeds”?

No, and treat anyone who does as a warning sign — Google’s own guidance on hiring an SEO names a guaranteed number one ranking as a reason to walk away. It is a heavily contested phrase, and for most local businesses it is not where the enquiries come from anyway.

What is within our control is structural, and it is included in the build: a page for each area and service you genuinely offer, service-area markup that matches your Google Business Profile, valid structured data, fast loading on mobile data, and internal links that let all of it be found.

Do you only work with businesses in these five cities?

No. We are a software business, so the work is not constrained by geography — we build for clients anywhere in the UK, and our published case studies run from north-west London to the West Midlands to Bedfordshire.

Leeds, Manchester, Birmingham, London and Edinburgh are the cities we focus on, which is why each has a page of its own. If you are somewhere else, nothing about the price, the process or the timeline changes.
